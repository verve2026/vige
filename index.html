Download for Mac Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 13+ · 127 MB

Install in three steps

  1. 1

    Download

    Press Download for Mac and wait for VIGE-mac-arm64.dmg to finish.

  2. 2

    Drag to Applications

    Open the downloaded file and drag VIGE onto the Applications folder next to it.

  3. 3

    Run setup once

    Open the Terminal app, paste the command below and press Return. It adds the tools and speech model VIGE needs to make videos, then opens the app.

curl -fsSL https://verve2026.github.io/vige/install.sh | bash -s -- --setup-only

Setup downloads the 1.6 GB speech model once and adds ffmpeg and whisper through Homebrew, so the first run takes a while. It never asks for your Mac password.

What your Mac needs

  • Apple SiliconA Mac with an M1 chip or newer, on macOS 13 or later.
  • HomebrewFree, from brew.sh. Setup uses it to add ffmpeg and whisper.
  • Google ChromeVIGE posts to TikTok through Chrome. You sign in to TikTok yourself.
  • Claude CodeYour own Claude subscription edits and writes captions. Without it VIGE falls back to a simpler editor.

Questions

Is VIGE safe to open?

Yes. VIGE is signed with an Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple (notarized), so macOS opens it without a warning. The first time, macOS asks once whether to open an app downloaded from the internet; press Open.

Do I have to run the setup command?

Yes, once per Mac. VIGE opens without it, but it cannot cut videos or write subtitles until ffmpeg, whisper and the speech model are installed. Settings > Setup check in the app shows what is missing.

Can I install with one command instead?

Yes. In Terminal, run curl -fsSL https://verve2026.github.io/vige/install.sh | bash. It downloads VIGE, puts it in Applications and does the setup in one go.

How do I update?

You do not need to. VIGE checks for a new version when it opens, downloads it in the background and shows a Restart to update button. Your accounts, videos and settings stay on your Mac. If you installed VIGE before auto-update existed (version 0.1.0), download it here once more and drag it into Applications.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your Mac. VIGE has no cloud and no shared server; each Mac has its own users and TikTok accounts.
